# AHH PEOPLE!!

Have you drunk something? Well... YOU GOTTA RUN AND AVOID PEOPLE!!

## About the Game

A chaotic platformer where avoiding people is the key to survival! Every encounter brings you closer to transforming into a monster, so keep your distance, watch the timer, and see how long you can last without losing yourself. Can you survive the crowd?

## Features

- **Avoid the Crowd** — Navigate levels while keeping your distance from other people.
- **Transformation Mechanic** — Every encounter brings you closer to becoming a monster.
- **Race Against Time** — Keep an eye on the timer as you try to survive.
- **Varied Characters** — Encounter different types of people throughout your run.
- **Level-Based Gameplay** — Navigate platforming challenges while avoiding encounters.
